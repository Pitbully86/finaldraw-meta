The math

It gets the numbers right — and shows its work.

Most certificates go out with the deductible double-counted or code items buried in depreciation, and carriers bounce them. FinalDraw knows ACV already includes the deductible, that recoverable depreciation is RCV minus ACV, and that paid-when-incurred code items sit outside it. The whole equation is on screen and turns green when it reconciles — before anything is sent.

The differentiator

Certify only the work you performed.

Did the roof, but not the siding or gutters? Certifying the whole claim asserts completion of work you didn't do. FinalDraw reads every line item off the estimate — each with its own RCV, depreciation and ACV — so you tick the items you performed and the certified figures are recalculated from exactly those. Exact addition, not estimation. If the items don't add up to the estimate's own stated total, FinalDraw refuses to guess.

Protection

The declined-work acknowledgement.

When a homeowner keeps the ACV money and skips part of the scope, the withheld depreciation on that work is forfeited and a future claim on it will likely be denied. FinalDraw generates a signed acknowledgement recording that they were told — listing the declined items, the dollars forfeited, and the consequences in plain words. The homeowner gets an informed choice in writing. You get proof you certified only what you built.

Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. $299 once, for the version you buy. Updates may be released at our discretion; major new versions may be paid upgrades.

Which carriers' estimates does it read?

Estimates printed through Xactimate — the format used by most major carriers — parse best, including full line-item detail. Other formats fall back to entering the figures by hand, and the certificate math still applies.

Does my claim data go to your servers?

No. FinalDraw stores everything on your own PC. The only network connections it makes are to an e-signature provider you configure with your own account.

Can I move it to a new computer?

Yes — remove the licence in the app, install on the new machine, and email us the new Installation ID for a replacement licence.

Is FinalDraw giving insurance or legal advice?

No. It prepares documents from figures you review and approve. You remain responsible for what you certify and for compliance with the laws of your state — see the licence agreement.
